My Approach

Beyond asking questions; creating opportunities for discovery. Children and teens do not always express their needs the way adults expect.

A questionnaire might tell us what they prefer today; but what do we know about things they haven't experienced yet?

My approach combines design thinking and creative participatory activities, allowing children to express their views through storytelling, drawing, creative challenges, and prototyping.
